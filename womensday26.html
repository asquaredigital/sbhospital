SB Hospital participated in a big way to celebrate International Women's Day 2026. As one of the main promoters of the Women's Day celebrations organised by "The Hindu" on 8th March 2026, SB Hospital reaffirmed its deep commitment to women's health, empowerment, and medical awareness across the Thanjavur region.

The Celebration

The event was a vibrant and meaningful celebration of womanhood, bringing together women from various walks of life in Thanjavur. SB Hospital was among the key promoters, reflecting its long-standing dedication to the well-being of women and families in the region.

The occasion served as a powerful platform to spread awareness on women's health issues, particularly around fertility and reproductive health — topics that often go undiscussed yet affect countless families across Tamil Nadu.

🌸 SB Hospital has been a pioneer in women's health care and Assisted Reproductive Technology in Thanjavur for over three decades.

Featured Speaker

Speaker

Dr. Manimalar

Head of Department — Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART)
SB Hospital, Thanjavur

Dr. Manimalar delivered a compelling and insightful speech on infertility awareness, stressing the importance of both partners being examined and investigated to identify the root cause of infertility in a couple. Her address highlighted that childlessness is a shared concern for both husband and wife, and that a complete evaluation of both is essential for an accurate diagnosis and successful treatment outcome.

Key Message from the Address

Breaking the Silence on Infertility

Dr. Manimalar's address brought a critical message to the forefront — infertility is not solely a woman's issue. Both partners must be evaluated thoroughly before arriving at a diagnosis. SB Hospital's Fertility Centre offers a comprehensive ART programme, ICMR approved, with advanced diagnostic and treatment facilities for both men and women.
